# Netlify visual reference

Source reviewed: https://www.netlify.com/

Useful high-level patterns for BuildABlock: editorial hero with a strong headline and clear primary/secondary CTAs; a dark hero illustration panel paired with a light, high-contrast navigation; strong visual separation between product sections; compact explanatory cards; and a clear conversion block near the end of the page. BuildABlock should borrow this hierarchy and confidence, not copy Netlify branding, logos, illustrations, or proprietary assets.

The BuildABlock adaptation uses its own black field-inspired palette, green contour-map visual, serif emphasis font, and land-planning content. Future imagery should be original or properly licensed.
